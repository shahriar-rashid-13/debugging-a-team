04 / FAILURE PROPAGATION
04 / 10

How one hidden mistake brings down the release.

The pyramid is the model. The chain reaction is why teams actually fail.

STEP 01 · ROOT
No Trust
A developer spots a flaw in their work—and stays quiet to look competent.
Mistake stays hidden
STEP 02 · SILENCE
No Conflict
In review, teammates sense risk but keep meetings polite instead of pushing back.
Artificial harmony
STEP 03 · DRIFT
No Commitment
Everyone nods in the meeting, then walks out unsure of the real plan.
Fake sign-off
STEP 04 · AVOIDANCE
No Accountability
Because nobody truly bought in, peers hesitate to call out slipped work.
Standards erode
STEP 05 · IMPACT
No Results
People protect personal reputation and tickets while the product misses the mark.
System failure
Like a distributed system with a broken base layer: you cannot patch results at the top while trust is broken at the bottom.
